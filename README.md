# Guidance Record and Status System

School counselors use this app to keep a private record for each student: who they are, what has happened, what the counselor and student are working toward, and what was said in each session. A status on the profile shows whether the student is cleared, being watched, in active intervention, referred outside the school, or closed.

## Records and statuses

Each student profile has four sections:

- **Student information** — name, grade, section, age, emergency contact.
- **Case history** — incidents, academic standing, family background, each with its own description. A case can have files attached (a scanned report, a letter).
- **Action plan** — goals the counselor and student set, such as attendance or anger management.
- **Session logs** — date, type (`routine`, `crisis`, `academic`), and a short note.

Status is one label on the student, plus a history of every change:

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Routine / Cleared | Doing well. Scheduled check-ins only. |
| Under Monitoring | A smaller ongoing issue. Regular follow-ups. |
| Critical / Intervention | Needs active counseling or crisis handling now. |
| Referred | Sent to an outside specialist or a higher school authority. |
| Case Closed | This issue is resolved. No immediate action. |

Changing a student to **Critical / Intervention** raises an alert automatically. The dashboard lists unacknowledged alerts, and a counselor can mark one acknowledged after they have seen it — nobody has to remember to flag it separately.

## Access and privacy

- **Counselor** — search students, open records, write cases, plans, sessions, and forms, change status, upload and print documents.
- **Admin** — same access, plus the ability to invite other staff.

There is no student or parent login. Parents receive a printed or downloaded call slip. A person can only sign in after a staff account has been set up for their email; anyone else has no access. Public sign-up stays off.

## Forms and the call slip

On a student record, **Create form** offers:

- **Call slip** — parent or guardian permission for the student to report to the guidance office at a scheduled time.
- **Waiver** — a short consent form with the student's information and signature lines.
- **Upload** — an existing file (a signed slip, a scanned waiver, any other case document) with a title and description.

Call slip, in order:

1. Counselor clicks **Create form**, then **Call slip**, on that student's record.
2. Counselor picks the date and time the student should come to the office.
3. The form fills itself in with the student's name, grade, section, age, and emergency contact. The counselor can correct the guardian name, and writes the reason for the call.
4. The app builds a one-page PDF: school and office name, student block, schedule, reason, and signature lines for the counselor, the guardian, and the student.
5. The counselor chooses **Print**, **Download**, **Attach to this record**, or any combination. Attaching stores the PDF securely and adds it to the student's documents list, tied to the call slip.

The waiver follows the same last step: preview, PDF, then print and/or attach.

## What's included

- **Sign in** — email and password.
- **Student list** — search by name or section; filter by status and by grade. Unacknowledged critical alerts sit at the top.
- **Reporting overview** — student counts and status shares by school year, grade, gender, and section, with a downloadable report.
- **Student record** — the four sections, the status control, documents, and **Create form**.
- **Call slip and waiver** — the flow above, opened from that student so the record is already chosen.
- **Alerts** — the same critical list, with acknowledge.
